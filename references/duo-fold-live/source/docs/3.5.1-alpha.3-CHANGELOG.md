- Restores the original perspective math, including top/bottom coordinates, blur and shading.
- V2 only limits horizontal sampling where perspective would squeeze content inward beyond its untransformed position. Once the original sampling returns inside that boundary, it passes through unchanged. No replacement camera, fixed-plane animation, or angle remapping.
- The same correction applies in reverse while closing, with rotation-aware axes and both renderers. Original remains selectable and unchanged.
- Preview layout, fades, display handoff, Keep Awake and the official 3.5.0 release are unchanged.
