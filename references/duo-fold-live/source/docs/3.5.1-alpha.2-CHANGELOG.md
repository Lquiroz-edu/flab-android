# 3.5.1-alpha.2 — V2 folding projection correction

- Replaces V2’s stationary texture mapping with a head-on, orthographic hinge projection. Folding motion returns; horizontal foreshortening follows the hinge in both directions.
- The original cover projection briefly samples about 2.1% beyond its full-width endpoint during initial unfolding, producing inward compression and a dark right edge. V2 starts at exactly full width and removes that depth-driven overshoot.
- Preserves hinge anchors, content crops, preview placement, reflection choice, blur, AA, fades and handoff timing. Original remains unchanged and is the default.
- Advanced → Screen placement → V2 Window Reveal applies to all animation styles and both glass renderers. No device-specific dimensions or direction-dependent timers are introduced.
- Validate on-device: compare closed → first opening frames, continue to the handoff, then reverse and close. This is a geometry experiment, not a fix for Samsung’s display blackout or Keep Awake.

3.5.0 remains the official release.
